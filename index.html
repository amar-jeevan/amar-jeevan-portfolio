About Me

I am a Master’s graduate in Electrical and Microsystems Engineering with a focus on simulation-driven engineering, numerical methods, and physics-based modeling.

My work centers on translating physical systems into computational models — combining thermal analysis, system-level thinking, and scientific computing to evaluate real engineering behavior under constraints.
