Cryogenic Cable Simulator

Thermal Modeling | Python | Numerical Methods

Project Overview

This project focuses on the thermal modeling of cryogenic cable systems under realistic cooling power constraints. The work was developed in the context of my Master’s thesis at Forschungszentrum Jülich.

What I Built

  • Python-based simulation framework for thermal conduction analysis
  • Temperature-dependent material modeling
  • Nonlinear steady-state solver
  • Multi-segment cable analysis with cooling constraints
  • Engineering plots for temperature and heat-flow behavior

Tools Used

Python, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, Voila, Jupyter

Engineering Relevance

The simulator helps evaluate thermal bottlenecks, operating points, and system behavior in cryogenic environments where cooling power is limited and material properties vary strongly with temperature.
